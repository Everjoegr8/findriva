Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Findriva is a service that helps businesses become easier to find, understand, trust and contact online. It is operated by ERA Technologiz, Lagos, Nigeria ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect when you use Findriva at findriva.eratechnologiz.com.ng, what we do with it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, a hashed password, and, if you turn it on, a two-factor sign-in secret. We never ask for or store the passwords of your other accounts.
  • Business information you enter: business name, categories, services, locations and map position, opening hours, phone numbers, website and social profile links, and the drafts, replies and pages you write or approve in Findriva.
  • Public website information: when you ask for an audit, we read the public pages of the website you give us, the same way a search engine does, and store what we find.
  • Data from accounts you connect: described in "Google user data" below.
  • Billing information: your plan, payment status and the reference our payment provider returns. Card and bank details are entered on Paystack's own pages and never reach our servers.
  • Usage and security records: sign-in times, IP address, browser type, and a log of important actions in your workspace (for example who approved a change), kept to protect your account and to show you an audit trail.

Google user data

You can choose to connect a Google account. You sign in on Google's own page and approve each permission there; we never see your Google password. You can disconnect at any time in Findriva, and you can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google product and permissionWhat we accessWhat we use it for
Google Search Console (read only, webmasters.readonly) The list of sites you can access, and search performance (searches, clicks, impressions, average position) and indexing status for the site you choose. Showing your results, trends and monthly reports, and confirming whether a fix has worked.
Google Business Profile (business.manage) Your business accounts and locations, the profile details of the location you choose, its customer reviews, and its daily performance figures (calls, direction requests, website clicks, views). Comparing your profile with your own business facts, showing your reviews and performance, and posting a reply to a review only after an owner or admin in your workspace approves that exact reply.
Basic profile (openid, email) The email address of the Google account you connected. Showing you which Google account is connected.

Bing Webmaster Tools (read only, webmaster.read): if you connect it, we read the list of your verified sites and, for the site you choose, Bing's daily clicks and impressions and weekly search queries, to show them next to your Google results. You can disconnect in Findriva or remove Findriva in Bing Webmaster Tools' settings; disconnecting deletes the data we imported.

We do not change your Google Business Profile, publish anything, or reply to reviews without that approval, and we never write to Google Search Console.

Limited Use. Findriva's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • we use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above;
  • we do not sell it, and we do not use or transfer it for advertising, including retargeting or personalised ads;
  • we do not use it to determine credit-worthiness or for lending;
  • we do not use it to develop, improve or train generalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models, and we do not send it to AI providers;
  • people at Findriva do not read it unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security or to investigate abuse, or the law requires it.

When you disconnect a Google account, we revoke our access with Google and delete the data we imported through that connection.

How we use information

  • To run the service: audits, action lists, keyword and ranking checks, content drafts, review replies, reports and your public business page.
  • To keep accounts secure, prevent abuse, and keep an audit trail of changes.
  • To bill you for your plan and send you service emails (sign-in links, password resets, reports and important changes). We do not send marketing emails without your consent.
  • To fix problems and improve Findriva using usage records.

Our legal bases are performing our contract with you, our legitimate interest in running a secure service, your consent where we ask for it (for example when you connect an account), and legal obligations.

Who we share information with

We do not sell personal data. We share only what each service provider needs to do its job for us:

  • Hosting and storage providers that run our servers, database and file storage.
  • Paystack to take payments in naira.
  • Resend to deliver our emails.
  • DataForSEO, which receives search keywords and locations (no personal data) to return search volumes, search results and map results.
  • Anthropic, which receives the customer questions you track and, if you use writing help, your approved business facts, to produce answers and drafts. It never receives Google user data.
  • Google and Microsoft Bing, when you connect those accounts, and search engines that support IndexNow, which receive the addresses of your public pages when you ask us to notify them.
  • Your own WordPress website, if you install the Findriva Connect plugin: it receives only the business details an owner or admin publishes to it. Findriva never signs in to your website.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect people and the service from harm.

Some of these providers process data outside Nigeria. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards the Nigeria Data Protection Act allows, such as contractual protections.

Your public business page

If you publish a business page, the details you approve for it (for example name, services, area, phone and opening hours) are public and can be read by anyone and by search engines. You can unpublish it at any time.

How long we keep information

  • Account and business data: while your account is open. If you delete a business or close your account, we delete its data within 30 days, except backups, which are overwritten within a further 30 days.
  • Data imported from connected accounts: until you disconnect, or until it is replaced by newer data.
  • Billing records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Security logs: up to 12 months.

Security

Connections are encrypted in transit. Tokens for connected accounts are encrypted in our database. Each workspace's data is kept separate, staff access is limited, and two-factor sign-in is available for every account.

Cookies and browser storage

Findriva stores a sign-in token and your chosen workspace in your browser so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, object to or restrict how we use it, or withdraw consent. Most of this you can do in Findriva yourself; for anything else, email us at the address below and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children

Findriva is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you by email or in Findriva before the change takes effect.
