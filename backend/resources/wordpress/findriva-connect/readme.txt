Shows the business details you approved in Findriva on your homepage.

== Description ==

Findriva Connect prints, on your homepage only:

* structured business data (JSON-LD) so search engines and AI assistants read your name, category, contact, location and hours reliably;
* a description and share preview (Open Graph) tags, unless an SEO plugin such as Yoast or Rank Math already prints them.

Nothing is printed until an owner or admin publishes it in Findriva. Findriva never logs in to WordPress and never asks for your password. Disconnecting in Findriva or here stops all output.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin and activate it.
2. In Findriva, open your business, then Website connection, and press "Create pairing code".
3. In WordPress, go to Settings, Findriva Connect, paste the code and press Connect.
